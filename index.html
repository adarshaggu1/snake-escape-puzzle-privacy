Snake Escape Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026
Publisher: Adarsh Kumar Aggu
Contact: digitalmarketingadarsh@gmail.com

This policy describes the Android game Snake Escape Puzzle (package
com.snakeescapepuzzle.game), version 1.2.1.

1. Summary

  • There is no account and no login. Your progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • The game contains no ads. It does not include an advertising SDK, third-party analytics or advertising-consent software.
  • The game needs an internet connection to play, and checks the connection regularly. Like any web request, the check reveals your device's IP address to the server it contacts (section 3).
  • An optional daily reminder is handled entirely on your device (section 2).
  • We, the publisher, do not run a server for this game and do not receive your game data.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves on your device: completed levels, stars, coins, hints, shop items,
awards, daily-challenge history, play statistics (such as puzzles solved and play
time) and your settings. When an update changes the save format, the game first
keeps a copy of your previous save on the device, so that your progress is not
lost.

Local gameplay log. The game keeps a log on your device of events such as
"level started", "level completed" or "hint used", with the time of each event, a
random number that changes every time the game starts, and short details such as
the level, the number of taps or mistakes and the time taken. It is used to
understand level difficulty. The log is limited in size; older entries are
discarded. The log is on by default; you can switch it off in
Settings → Privacy & data → Local gameplay log. The game does not send the log
anywhere.

Daily puzzle reminder (optional). The reminder is off until you turn it on in
Settings → Reminders (or accept the game's one-time offer after a daily
puzzle). On Android 13 and later, the game asks Android for permission to show
notifications only at that moment, never at launch. When it is on:

  • Android shows at most one notification a day, usually within about half an hour after the time you choose (9:00, 13:00, 18:00 or 20:00). Android can deliver it later, for example to save battery; the game skips a reminder that arrives more than about two hours late, and never shows one between 21:00 and 08:00;
  • no reminder is shown on a day you have already solved the daily puzzle;
  • tapping the notification opens the game and takes you to the Daily screen, unless you are in the middle of a puzzle;
  • the reminder is set up again after the phone restarts, after the game is updated, and when the device's time or time zone changes.

To do this, the game stores on your device whether the reminder is on, the chosen
time, the notification text, the date you last solved the daily puzzle, and
whether the reminder offer has been shown or declined. The reminder is scheduled
by Android on the device: no notification server is used,
no notification token is created and nothing is sent anywhere. Turn it off in
Settings → Reminders or in Android's notification settings for the game.

Deleting your data. Settings → Reset progress erases your progress, the
game's backup copies of it (including copies kept from earlier versions) and the
local gameplay log (your settings, including the reminder choice, are kept).
Uninstalling the game removes all of its data from your device.

3. Connection check

The game can only be played online. At launch, when you return to the game,
before a puzzle starts (if the last successful check is more than a minute old)
and about every 30 seconds while a puzzle is open, it makes a small HTTPS request
to Google's public connectivity-check address
(connectivitycheck.gstatic.com/generate_204). If a check fails, the game may
repeat it once, then asks you to connect; each time you tap Retry it checks
again.

What the request reveals. The game does not put your progress, settings,
gameplay log or any account or advertising identifier into the request. Like any
web request, it does reveal to Google, which operates that address:

  • your device's IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • the time of the request;
  • standard technical information that accompanies web requests, such as request headers that can identify the kind of software making the request.

How Google handles information it receives is described in
Google's Privacy Policy.

Other network use by the game. If you tap Open full document in the
game's Privacy screen, your browser opens this page, which is hosted on GitHub
Pages; like any website, GitHub receives your IP address when the page loads. We
checked the game's code and the software libraries packaged with it for other
network use and found none that this version uses.

4. Advertising

This version of the game contains no ads. It does not include the Google
Mobile Ads SDK, Google's consent SDK or any other advertising SDK, does not
request or show ads, does not use the Android advertising ID and does not show a
consent form for advertising. If a future version adds ads, this Privacy Policy
will be updated before that version is released.

5. Android and Google Play

Android and Google Play on your device communicate with Google on their own, for
example to install and update apps. That communication happens independently of
this game: the game does not start, control or receive it, and it is not
described in this policy. Information about it is available from Google and from
your device's manufacturer.

6. Children and teens

Snake Escape Puzzle is intended for a general audience of teens and adults. It is
not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13. The game's data is stored on the device: to
delete progress, use Settings → Reset progress; to delete all of the game's
data, uninstall the game.

7. Permissions

  • Internet (INTERNET) - to play online and check the connection (section 3).
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, asked on Android 13 and later) - only if you turn on the daily reminder.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) - to set the daily reminder again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE) - for optional vibration and haptic feedback.
