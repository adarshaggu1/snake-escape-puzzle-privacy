Snake Escape Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026
Publisher: Adarsh Kumar Aggu
Contact: digitalmarketingadarsh@gmail.com

This policy describes the Android game Snake Escape Puzzle (package
com.snakeescapepuzzle.game), version 1.0.0.

1. Summary

  • There is no account and no login. Your progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • The game needs an internet connection to play, and checks the connection regularly (section 3).
  • The game contains optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad technology is started, and an ad is requested, only when you choose to watch an ad, and only if your consent choice allows it (sections 4-5).
  • Each time you open the game, it asks Google's consent service whether consent is needed in your region (section 5).
  • We, the publisher, do not run a server for this game and do not receive your game data.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves on your device: completed levels, stars, coins, hints, shop items,
awards, daily-challenge history and your settings.

Local gameplay log. The game can keep a log on your device of events such as
"level started", "level completed" or "hint used", to understand level
difficulty. It never leaves your device. You can switch it off in
Settings → Privacy & data → Local gameplay log.

Daily puzzle reminder (optional). If you turn the reminder on, the game asks
Android for permission to show notifications and schedules one local reminder a
day. It stores the chosen time and whether you finished that day's puzzle on your
device. No notification server is used and nothing is sent anywhere. Turn it off
in Settings → Reminders or in Android's notification settings.

Deleting your data. Settings → Reset progress erases your progress and the
local gameplay log (your settings are kept). Uninstalling the game removes all of
its data from your device.

3. Connection check

The game can only be played online. At launch, when you return to the game,
before a puzzle starts and about every 30 seconds while you play, it makes a
small HTTPS request to Google's public connectivity-check address
(connectivitycheck.gstatic.com/generate_204). The request contains no game data
and no identifiers from the game; like any web request, it reveals your device's
IP address to Google. If the check fails, the game asks you to connect and try
again.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (GMA Next-Gen SDK) to show **rewarded ads
only** - no banners and no interstitial ads. An ad is requested only after you
choose to watch one (for example for a hint, an extra heart or bonus coins), and
you receive the reward only if Google confirms the ad was watched.

What Google collects. When the ads SDK is started and an ad is requested or
shown, Google's SDK automatically collects and shares the following with Google
for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in Google's
data disclosure for the GMA Next-Gen SDK:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device;
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit. How Google uses it:
How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Ad content. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences
(rating "G").

Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in
your device's Android settings.

5. Consent and privacy choices

Each time you open the game, it uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check
whether consent is needed in your region; this request reaches Google even if you
never watch an ad. Where the law requires it (for example in the European
Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown before
the ads SDK is started or any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any
time in Settings → Privacy & data → Manage ad consent (shown where your region
requires it).

6. Children and teens

Snake Escape Puzzle is intended for a general audience of teens and adults. It is
not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13. All game data stays on the device: to delete
it, use Settings → Reset progress or uninstall the game; the advertising ID can
be reset or deleted in Android settings.

7. Permissions

  • Internet / network state - to play online, check the connection and show ads.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later) - only if you turn on the daily reminder.
  • Run at startup - to restore the daily reminder after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration - for optional haptic feedback.
  • Advertising ID - used by Google's ads SDK (section 4).
